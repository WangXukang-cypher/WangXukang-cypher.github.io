MULTIMODAL & FOUNDATION MODEL RESEARCH · 2026

Learning how intelligent models perceive, reason, and adapt.

I am a researcher from Chongqing, currently based in Tokyo. With academic training in urban planning, information design, and spatial information, I now study multimodal learning, generative models, and the training and post-training algorithms behind LLMs and VLMs.

I care most about capable, efficient, and generalizable algorithms. Systems and accelerator constraints are part of the research context: they help turn algorithmic ideas into models that can be trained, evaluated, and deployed at scale.

Current research agenda

Algorithms first, systems aware

My central interest is how modern models learn, reason, generate, and generalize. I also study the training systems and hardware constraints that make these algorithms practical at scale.

Foundation-Model Algorithms & Learning

Studying how LLMs and VLMs acquire capabilities through attention, long-context modeling, MoE, representation learning, data mixtures, optimization objectives, and efficient operators.

LLM / VLMMoERepresentation learning

Post-Training & Generalization

Exploring supervised fine-tuning, distillation, preference optimization, reinforcement learning, curriculum and data design, with attention to stability, forgetting, transfer, and reliable evaluation.

SFT / RLDistillationPreference learning

Scalable Training & Hardware-Aware AI

Investigating multi-node parallelism, mixed precision, communication–computation overlap, KV-cache and serving optimization, and the algorithm–system co-design needed for reliable cross-accelerator adaptation.

DP / TP / PP / EPKV CacheCross-accelerator

Multimodal Generation & Embodied Intelligence

Following unified image–video–audio generation, vision-language-action policies, world-action prediction, embodied reasoning, simulation, real-time control, and the path from large-scale pretraining to on-device deployment.
